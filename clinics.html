FAQ

Clinic Questions

Can I sign up for just one Sunday?

Yes. You can register à la carte for individual sessions or save by signing up for the full series. Choose your sessions at checkout.

What ages are the Fall Clinics for?

Boys and girls ages 6–14, all levels welcome. If you're unsure whether it's the right fit for your player, just email us.

Where are the clinics held?

The Kobe Bryant Gymnasium at Lower Merion High School, 315 E. Montgomery Ave, Ardmore, PA 19003. Park in the lot right by the gymnasium and come in through the gym entrance.

Do clinics sell out?

Yes — our fall and spring clinics sell out every year. We recommend registering as early as possible to secure a spot.

Is financial aid available?

Yes. We never want cost to keep a kid off the court — email aceshoops@gmail.com and we'll take care of it privately.

What is your refund / cancellation policy?

Registration fees are non-refundable, but if your player is injured or has to withdraw we'll issue a credit toward a future DBA clinic — just email aceshoops@gmail.com. If we ever cancel a session, that session is credited or refunded. Full details on our policies page.

How do drop-off and pick-up work?

Please arrive about 10 minutes early and check in with our staff at the gym door. Sessions end at 12:15 PM, and players are released to a parent or to the authorized pickup person you list on the registration form.

Can I sign up after the series has started?

Yes. Single Sundays stay open right up until each session, so you can join for any remaining dates — just pick the ones you want on the registration form.
