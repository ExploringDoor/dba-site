POP-UP FLYER IMAGE GOES HERE
============================

Drop your flyer image into this folder and name it exactly:

    flyer.jpg      (or flyer.png)

That's it. The moment the file is here and pushed live, the pop-up
appears automatically on the homepage. Until then, the site behaves
normally with no pop-up.

Tips for the image:
- Portrait (tall) works best, like a real flyer. ~1000 x 1400 px is plenty.
- Keep it under ~1 MB so it loads fast on phones (a JPG is smaller than a PNG).
- The whole image is clickable and takes people to the registration page,
  and there are also two buttons underneath: "Register for Fall Clinics" and
  "Enter site".
